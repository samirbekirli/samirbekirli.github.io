Questions about my journey, projects, and how to collaborate.

Hi, I’m Samir Bekirli. I’m a software developer and entrepreneur building web applications, SaaS products, and AI-powered tools. I’m the founder of Mitfly, an intelligent flight search and comparison platform, and the creator of WidGefter, a YouTube channel where I share startup journeys and software engineering insights.
Mitfly is a next-generation flight search and comparison platform engineered to simplify travel planning. It allows travelers to analyze the best flight options across hundreds of airlines and booking agencies in seconds, saving both valuable time and budget through intelligent filtering and price insights.
Mitfly is slated for its official public release in 2027. We are currently actively developing the core flight search engine, data integrations, and fine-tuning the user experience for peak performance.
Yes! Mitfly will offer both free and premium plans. Core flight search, route comparison, and standard filtering will be completely free for everyone. Premium tiers will provide real-time price drop alerts, predictive fare analytics, and personalized route recommendations.
Yes, you can definitely join us! We’d love to collaborate and build Mitfly into a world-class travel platform together. Whether you are into software engineering, product design, or marketing, our doors are open.
