Mitfly ✈️

A flight search and comparison platform designed to make travel planning easier.

What Mitfly is

Mitfly helps travelers search for flights and compare their options in one place, so planning a trip takes less time and fewer open tabs.

Status

Mitfly is currently in development. Updates are shared on the WidGefter YouTube channel and on this site.

Who is behind it

Mitfly is founded and built by Samir Bekirli, a developer who also runs the WidGefter YouTube channel about entrepreneurship, startups, and SaaS development.
